Student Error: Intro Comma - English
✍️ COMMA MISTAKE #1 — almost everyone makes this!
❌ WRONG: "Although she was tired she kept studying."
✅ CORRECT: "Although she was tired, she kept studying."
WHY? When a sentence begins with an introductory clause (Although…, Because…, Since…, While…), you MUST put a comma after it.
That comma shows where the introduction ends and main idea begins. Without it, sentence feels rushed — and costs you points in TOEFL/IELTS.
Another example:
❌ "Because it was raining we stayed home."
✅ "Because it was raining, we stayed home."
Note: When clause comes AFTER main clause, no comma needed:
✅ "She kept studying although she was tired."
