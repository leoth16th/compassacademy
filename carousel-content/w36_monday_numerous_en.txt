Word: Numerous (Collocation) - English
Slide 1: Tired of saying MANY? Upgrade with NUMEROUS — the word examiners LOVE to see. 🚀
Slide 2: numerous (adj.) = great in number; many. Formal, C1, powerful. Example: "She has made numerous contributions to the project."
Slide 3: Word family: numerous (adj.) → numerously (adv.) → numerousness (n.)
Slide 4: Collocation 1: numerous occasions - "On numerous occasions, the committee failed to reach an agreement."
Slide 5: Collocation 2: numerous attempts - "After numerous attempts, the researchers finally succeeded."
Slide 6: Collocation 3: numerous ways - "There are numerous ways to reduce stress."
Slide 7: Formality: several (3-5) | many (informal) | numerous (FORMAL) 🎯
Slide 8: TOEFL/IELTS advantage: Examiners reward Lexical Resource. Using "numerous" signals C1+ vocabulary.
