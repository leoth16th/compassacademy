TOEFL Reading Part 6 Strategy (English)
Slide 1: Stuck on TOEFL Reading? These 10 strategies will change how you attack every passage. 🎯
Slide 2: Read the first sentence of each paragraph. The main idea summarizes the WHOLE passage — not just one paragraph.
Slide 3: Wrong answers often repeat exact words from the text but twist the meaning. If an option looks like a copy-paste, check it twice.
Slide 4: Test writers love flipping meaning. If an answer says "increased" but the passage says "decreased" — it's a trap.
Slide 5: Spend max 20 minutes per Reading passage. That's about 1 minute 20 seconds per question. Clock it.
Slide 6: 10 minutes a day beats 3 hours once a week. Consistency rewires your brain.
Slide 7: In multi-select questions, exactly 3 answers are correct. Count them. No more, no less.
Slide 8: Cross out obviously wrong options first. You don't need to find the right answer — you need to remove the wrong ones.
Slide 9: Skim the questions BEFORE the passage. Your brain will hunt for answers as you read.
