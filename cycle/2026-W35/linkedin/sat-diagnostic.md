# Saturday Diagnostic Post

**Can you spot the error?**

> "I'm *looking forward to meet* you tomorrow."

The mistake: *looking forward to* is followed by a **gerund (-ing form)**, not an infinitive.

✅ "I'm looking forward to **meeting** you tomorrow."
❌ "I'm looking forward to **meet** you tomorrow."

Why? *To* here is a preposition, not part of an infinitive. After prepositions, verbs take the -ing form.

Other examples:
- *accustomed to **working** late*
- *committed to **improving***
- *opposed to **changing** the rules*
