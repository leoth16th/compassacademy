# Monday Collocation Post

**Collocation of the Week:** *make a decision*

Don't say "do a decision" — the correct verb is **make**.

✅ *She made a quick decision.*
❌ *She did a quick decision.*

Other common collocations with *decision*:
- reach a decision
- take a decision (BrE)
- finalize a decision
- defer a decision
